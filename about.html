Contact Us
  • Do you supply industrial wheels for factories and warehouses?
    Yes. We supply a wide range of industrial wheels for factories, warehouses and production lines, in different sizes and materials to suit every application.

    All our products are original and imported from Wicke, the German manufacturer known for its high quality and advanced industrial standards.
